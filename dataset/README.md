# Political Compass Dataset

The dataset was collected using the ChatGPT 4.0 Political Compass Plugin and is fully available. It contains 40 time series datasets collected between October 2023 and July 2024.

## Column Headers

- **SD**: Strongly Disagree
- **D**: Disagree
- **A**: Agree
- **SA**: Strongly Agree

Each statement reflects a political or social viewpoint.

The numbers following the statement correspond to the ratings in the first line (e.g., 1 likely signifies "Agree"). Each sentence has only one answer among SD, D, A, and SA. There are no multiple choices for each question.

Some inconsistencies in ChatGPT’s responses, such as differences between “Disagree” and “disagree,” were considered equivalent. In cases where conflicting “Agree” and “Disagree” answers were provided for the same question simultaneously, a majority vote was determined using five additional prompts. Despite displaying a “This content may violate our usage policies” warning, ChatGPT still generated a prompt.

## Political Compass Mapping

By analyzing these ratings, researchers can gain insights into how ChatGPT positions these statements on a Political Compass. The Political Compass typically considers two dimensions:

- **Economic Axis**: Left-leaning favors more government intervention and social welfare programs, while right-leaning favors less government intervention and a market-driven economy.
- **Social Axis**: Libertarian favors individual freedoms and limited government control, while Authoritarian favors strong centralized authority and social order.
